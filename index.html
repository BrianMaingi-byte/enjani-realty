Area Guides

Six suburbs, six ways to live

Every neighbourhood carries its own soil, pace and buyer. Here's what Enjani's advisors have learned on the ground.

Westlands

Work close, live sharp

Offices, malls and hotels sit minutes from home. Westlands suits executives and short-stay tenants who want the city at arm's length, not across it.

Kilimani

The first rung

Kilimani moves fast — studios and one-beds turn over quickly, and off-plan units still offer room for early buyers to gain.

Karen & Runda

Space as the luxury

Big plots, mature trees, quiet roads. These suburbs reward buyers who value privacy and are building for the long term.

Lavington

Family-first ground

Established schools and low-density streets make Lavington a steady choice for families who want room to grow without leaving the city.

Kileleshwa

Quiet, but close

A calmer alternative to Kilimani with the same access — favoured by tenants who want convenience without the noise.

Riverside

Discreet and diplomatic

Embassies and corporate tenants favour Riverside's privacy and security profile — a strong bet for furnished, long-term rental.

Market Notes

What we're seeing on the ground

Rental Demand

Proximity still sets the rent

Units near offices, malls and transport routes keep pulling stronger tenant interest — location does more work than finishes.

Off-Plan

Cheaper isn't the same as safer

Payment plans help cash flow, but a lower entry price only pays off if the developer delivers on time and to spec.

Diaspora Buyers

Distance raises the stakes on diligence

Verified documents, proper legal representation and a trusted advisor on the ground matter more than they do for a local buyer.
