Advisors who know the ground they sell

Enjani Realty was started on a simple idea: a property is only as good as the ground it sits on, and Nairobi has more variety in that ground than most buyers realise. We work six suburbs closely rather than the whole city thinly, so every recommendation comes from an advisor who has actually walked the block, not just read the listing.

PRINCIPAL BROKER

Leadership

Amani Wanjiru, Principal Broker

Amani leads Enjani Realty's advisory team, with a background in urban land economics and over 400 transactions closed across Nairobi's prime suburbs since founding the firm. She built Enjani around a research-first approach — every listing is checked against title, zoning and comparable sales before it reaches a client.

Enjani's advisors are known for straight answers on pricing and timeline, even when that means telling a client to wait.
